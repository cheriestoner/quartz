## Objectives
---
_This is a formative, generative study. The prototype is not being evaluated. The goal is to produce hypotheses, design insights, and a tested protocol._
1. Explore embodied and sensorimotor engagement
	- To explore how dynamic spectrogram visualisation may engage sensorimotor and embodied processes––both in relation to one's own or others' voice
	- To generate hypotheses about whether it can substitute for or augment auditory motor simulation
2. Inform future interface design
	To identify design opportunities for the live spectrogram interface that better support sensorimotor and embodied processes in voice perception, based on participants' self-reported feedback and behavioural response.
3. Inform future quantitative experiment
	Test task designs for future experiments
