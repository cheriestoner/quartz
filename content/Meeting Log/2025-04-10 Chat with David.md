PhD Structure & Academic Expectations

- What are the publication requirements for the PhD degree?
- Is a comprehensive thesis expected, or a compilation of journal articles?
- Which journals or conferences does your lab typically publish in?
- How often do your PhD students present at international conferences?

Supervision & Daily Research

- Will I be working more closely with you or with Kasia on a day-to-day basis?
- What is the PhD student's role in this project?
- Are there any existing datasets or pilot findings the project will build on?
- How much room is there for developing my own direction within the project?
- Are there regular lab meetings or other structures for collaboration and support?

Research Environment & Opportunities

- Can I collaborate on other ongoing research in the lab (e.g., bioacoustics, soundscapes)?
- Do you see potential intersections with HCI, interactive systems, or computing?
- Are there internal or external opportunities for interdisciplinary collaborations?

Teaching, Mobility & Career Development

- Will I have teaching responsibilities during the PhD?
- How are international secondments structured? Do you have ongoing partnerships with UCL or Cochlear at the moment?
- What are typical career paths for your PhD graduates?

Funding, Timeline & Practicalities

- What is the expected start date?
- Have you had previous Marie-Curie funded PhD students?
- What is the funding structure, and is there a way to extend beyond 3 years if needed?
- Are there institutional resources (e.g., travel grants, career support, language training)?
