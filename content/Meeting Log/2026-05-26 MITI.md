Data preparation:

- Data augmentation -- when data is scarce & increase robustness

Pretraining
- UrbanSound8k
- Donate a cry
- Seeing sound dataset (small)

3 models, pretrained: ResNet18 VGG MobileNetV3-Small
Fine-tuning strategy:
- replace the last layer with a single neuron for binary classification
- freeze the rest layers
- fixed validation test vs 5-folds (5-folds is better for small dataset as the model is trained on the whole dataset)
