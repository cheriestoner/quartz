## Minimal Viable Experiment: Live Spectrogram for Embodied Vocal Learning
based on sensorimotor theory

**Goal:** Users produce sounds while seeing a live visual representation of their voice to learn and improve vocal control (especially relevant for deaf users).
1. **Variables (what to manipulate)**
    - Responsiveness of the visual feedback
    - Feedback delay
    - Type of acoustic feature displayed (pitch, timbre, amplitude, etc.)
2. **Conditions (comparisons for experiments)**
    - Real-time responsive mapping vs delayed/fixed mapping
    - Simple vs complex feature mappings
    - Different levels of interaction freedom
3. **Measurable Behaviors (what to observe)**
    - Changes in vocal production accuracy
    - Learning speed / adaptation curve
    - Subjective reports (“I can feel the change”)
