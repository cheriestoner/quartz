1. **Label-to-image matching**: Instead of labeling a single spectrogram, participants are shown 5 spectrograms and asked to select the one that matches a given semantic label (e.g. "which of these is a scream?"). This removes the need for participants to mentally visualize what a category "should look like", they only need to compare images relative to each other.

2. **Audio-image matching** (as discussed before): A sound is played, and participants judge whether it matches a displayed spectrogram. This bypasses semantic labels entirely — participants use the sound itself as the reference rather than a mental image of a category.
