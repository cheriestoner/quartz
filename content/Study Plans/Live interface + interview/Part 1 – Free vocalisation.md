## Task 1: Vocalisation with real-time visual feedback
Participants vocalise freely while observing live spectrogram feedback of their own voice.

Suggested vocalisations (encourage free exploration too):
- Sustained vowels
- Nonverbal vocalisations: laugh, cry, scream, moan, roar, etc.
- Singing
- Speech

Play with params, explorative
## Interview Questions
#### Task-specific
- What did you notice about your voice while watching the spectrogram?
- Which kind of vocalisation aligned the best with the visualization?
- Did seeing the feedback change how you vocalised, or how it felt?
- Any moments where you felt the visualisation helped or interfered?
- Was the visualisation informative, distracting, or something else?
#### General / closing
- Was there anything about the interface that made you want to explore further?
- What would make the visualisation more useful or more engaging?
- Any moments where the visual and auditory felt connected — or disconnected?


RQs
- does seeing one’s voice alter the felt experience of vocalising?
- how did participants adjusted the parameters, for which vocalisations?
- Which features of the dynamic spectrogram do participants actually respond to — motion, colour, rhythm, shape?
- Does the expert/naive distinction reflect learned decoding or more direct motor resonance?
- Which stimulus type produces the clearest embodied response?
