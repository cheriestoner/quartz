[[Study 1 & 2 – Experiment protocol]]

| **Hypothesis**                                          | **DV type** | **IV type**                 | **Recommended model**                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Accuracy > chance (single condition)                    | Proportion  | None                        | **One-sample proportion test** or **binomial test**                                                                         |
| Accuracy differs between 3 sound types (within-subject) | Proportion  | 3 levels, repeated measures | **Repeated-measures ANOVA** (on arcsin-transformed proportions) or **mixed-effects logistic regression**                    |
| Accuracy differs between groups (between-subjects)      | Proportion  | 2+ groups                   | **Independent t-test** (two groups) or **ANOVA** (more than two groups), optionally logistic regression if trial-level data |
