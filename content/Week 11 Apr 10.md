## Readings:
**Reading list:**
-  [How passive is passive listening? Toward a sensorimotor theory of auditory perception](http://link.springer.com/10.1007/s11097-019-09641-6)
- [Sensorimotor Integration Can Enhance Auditory Perception](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-58447-z)
**take-aways:**
- Discussion and applications of sensorimotor theory on auditory perception is relatively scarce, compared to visual and tactile modality that are inherently more active, explorative, object-oriented – auditory perception is often considered peripheral and more "passive"
- Relevant papers above provided neural evidence on *motor activation* during listening (from EEG & fMRI studies)
- Listening is also a *bodily activity*, but need to consider differences between *action-related* (sound produced from an action) and *action-soliciting* (sounds inviting or cuing a action in response) *sounds*
